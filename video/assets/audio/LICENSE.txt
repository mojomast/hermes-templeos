ONE LIVE SCREEN — score, effects and narration

Score
  Original composition and arrangement for this submission, rendered with
  MorningDAW 1.6.3 (Morningdew Media AB, proprietary freeware, used as a tool;
  not redistributed). Instruments are played through Surge XT (GPL-3.0, used
  as a tool). Reverb is Dragonfly Hall and Dragonfly Room (GPL-3.0). Tape
  colour on the master is Airwindows ToTape6 (MIT). Factory drum samples are
  referenced from a local MorningDAW install and are not copied into this
  repository. The .mdproject and spec live in morningdaw/. Rebuild with
  scripts/music.py; you need your own MorningDAW install.

  The WAV of the score is an original composition. It is not a CC0 dedication,
  because it includes MorningDAW factory drum sounds used as played parts.

SFX
  Original synthesized typing, power and glitch effects, created in Python
  for this submission, with no third-party samples. Dedicated to the public
  domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Narration
  Generated with stock Kokoro-82M v1.0 (full-precision fp32 ONNX) voices:
  a 60/40 blend of af_heart and af_bella. This is not a clone or imitation of
  Terry A. Davis. The script is original. Generated WAVs are bundled; model
  weights are not. Kokoro-82M weights are Apache 2.0; the kokoro-onnx wrapper
  is MIT.
  https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
  https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx
